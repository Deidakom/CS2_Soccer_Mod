[h1]CS:2 - SoccerMod Feature Package[/h1]

Everything the KA Soccer Mod server needs on your side: menu, jerseys, sounds, voice calls, minimap and HUD. [b]Subscribe and restart CS2[/b] - without this item most SoccerMod features are missing on the server.

[h2]What's inside[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Clickable SoccerMod menu[/b] (!menu) with settings, help and a full command list
[*][b]Football kits[/b] for both teams and goalkeepers
[*][b]Football calls[/b] on V with voice lines: Well done!, Pass here!, Cross!, I'm free!, Pass back!, Nice pass!, Sorry!
[*][b]Sounds[/b]: ball kick, goal net, post and crossbar hits, sprint breathing, stadium whistles, air horn, cheering and booing - each can be switched off in Settings - Sounds
[*][b]Sprint bar[/b] at the bottom of the screen (SPRINT / RECHARGE / READY with percentage)
[*][b]Minimap[/b]: an aerial view of the stadium
[*][b]Clean stadium[/b]: logos and URLs removed from the pitch
[*][b]3D grass[/b] on the stadium pitch - switch it on or off for yourself in !menu - Settings - Visuals
[*][b]Match scoreboard[/b] at the top of the screen (teams, score, clock, period) with kick-off, goal, half-time and full-time banners, and a soccer [b]TAB board[/b]
[*]Silent knife on the ball (our own kick sound instead) and no shards, debris or dust when you hit it
[/list]

[h2]Help[/h2]
In game: [b]!menu[/b] opens the menu, [b]!links[/b] prints this link to your console, [b]!binds[/b] prints the menu key binds. Missing or old content? Close CS2, let Steam finish the Workshop download, then start CS2 again.
